Every year we close the school year by looking back carefully. These are some of the numbers from 2025/26.

## Mentoring

- **801 hours** of mentoring, recorded session by session in our attendance file.
- **34 mentors and 34 mentees** active at the end of the year, across five neighbourhoods.
- **85%** attendance for volunteers and **83%** for mentees.

This year we started counting hours from real attendance instead of estimates. The numbers got smaller, but also more honest.

## Psychosocial activities

Five outings throughout the year:

1. Treasure hunt and show at the Puppet Museum (November)
2. Climbing at Escalada 25 (February)
3. Physics Circus with NFIST (March)
4. Lisbon Zoo (May)
5. UP Day with the São João da Talha Rugby School (June)

## What we’ll change

Our surveys showed that many young people enjoy the activities, but turnout was lower than we wanted. Next year we’ll get mentors more involved in spreading the word and ask mentees what they’d like to do.

Thank you to every volunteer, partner and sponsor who made this year possible.
