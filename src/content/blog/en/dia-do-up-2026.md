On 7 June we brought together young people and volunteers from every neighbourhood at **Jardim Álvaro Roxo**, in São João da Talha, for UP Day. It’s how we celebrate the end of the school year, and probably the most awaited day of all.

## A day outdoors

The São João da Talha Rugby School, our partner since 2022, prepared a series of games and challenges: agility, decision-making and lots of teamwork. Some discovered a talent for rugby they didn’t know they had.

Lunch was shared: everyone brought something, and the table filled up.

## Reading champions

At the end of the afternoon we handed out the **Reading Game** awards, the board game we created to get mentees reading more. Congratulations to every reader, and to the mentors who cheered them on week after week.

See you in September!
