March was a busy month. Here are the three things that stood out.

## We arrived in Damaia

We made our partnership with **Centro Social 6 de Maio** in Damaia official. Mentoring pairs are already meeting every week, and more young people are waiting for a mentor. If you live or study nearby, [now is a great time to join](/en/volunteer/).

## A new look

We launched UP’s new visual identity. The balloon is still there, because that’s what we do: help every young person rise a little higher. Now it comes with colours, textures and strokes that feel more like us.

## Training with Teach For Portugal

On 7 March, 14 volunteers spent the afternoon at Casa do Impacto in a workshop run by **Teach For Portugal**. We talked about teaching strategies, building relationships with mentees and handling the harder moments. The evening ended, of course, with dinner.

Thank you to everyone who made this month happen.
