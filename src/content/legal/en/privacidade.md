Associação UP Desenvolvimento respects the privacy of the users of its website and is committed to protecting the personal data shared with us.

*This is a translation for convenience; the [Portuguese version](/politica-de-privacidade/) prevails.*

## 1. Data controller

The entity responsible for processing personal data is **Associação UP Desenvolvimento**, tax number (NIF) 517 259 532.

For any question about personal data, you can contact us at: [up.desenvolvimento.geral@gmail.com](mailto:up.desenvolvimento.geral@gmail.com)

## 2. Personal data collected

This website has no user accounts and no forms of its own. Your data only reaches us when you email us or fill in one of our application forms (Google Forms), which the website links to. In those cases, we may collect:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number (where applicable)
- Information submitted through forms (e.g. volunteer applications or contact requests)

## 3. Purpose of processing

The personal data collected is used to:

- Reply to contact requests
- Process volunteer applications
- Send communications about the association’s activities (when authorised)

## 4. Legal basis

The processing of personal data is based on:

- The user’s consent (e.g. forms)
- The need to reply to requests made by the user

## 5. Data sharing

Data may be processed by external platforms used by Associação UP Desenvolvimento, acting as processors under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **Vercel Inc.**, which hosts the website
- **Google** (Google Forms and Gmail), used for the application forms and email

Associação UP Desenvolvimento **does not sell or share personal data for commercial purposes.**

## 6. Data transfers

Some data may be processed outside the European Economic Area, namely through the services listed above, ensuring that the appropriate data protection safeguards required by the GDPR are in place.

## 7. Data retention

Personal data is kept only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or until the user requests its deletion.

## 8. Your rights

Under applicable law, you have the right to:

- Access your personal data
- Request its correction
- Request its deletion
- Withdraw your consent to processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese Data Protection Authority, CNPD ([cnpd.pt](https://www.cnpd.pt)).

## 9. Cookies

This website **does not use cookies** or any tracking or advertising tools. Fonts are hosted on the website itself, with no requests to third-party services. For security reasons, the hosting provider may log technical visit data (such as IP addresses).

Links to social networks (Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook) and to the forms lead to third-party websites, which have their own privacy policies.

## 10. Security

Associação UP Desenvolvimento takes appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse or unauthorised access.

## 11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

Associação UP Desenvolvimento may update this Privacy Policy whenever necessary. We recommend checking this page regularly.
